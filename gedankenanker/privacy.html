Privacy Policy

GedankenAnker for Android · Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

GedankenAnker is a note-taking app. Everything you write down in it stays on your device. The app has no access to the internet, needs no account, shows no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services; there are no servers of ours behind it. We do not learn what you note down. Your data leaves your device only when you share it yourself, when Android includes it in the device backup of your Google account or when you transfer it to a new device while setting it up. We process personal data only when you buy the app on Google Play or write to us. The details follow below.

Your data in the app

Thoughts, lists, collections, dates, reminders, settings and the attachments of your thoughts (photos, files, drawings) are stored by the app only on your device, in its own storage area protected by Android. Other apps can reach this data only when you yourself open or share something with them. The app sends none of it to us or to anyone else. We do not process this data and can neither see nor restore it.

This storage on your device is strictly necessary for the app to work and therefore needs no consent (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG).

What you delete stays in the app's trash for 30 days and is then deleted for good; before that you can restore it there or delete it for good right away. Completed thoughts are kept unless you choose otherwise in the settings ("Delete completed automatically"). All of the app's data is gone when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings; a device backup is not affected by this (see below).

Permissions

The app asks only for what it needs for reminders: showing notifications, reminding you to the minute ("Alarms and reminders"), rescheduling the reminders after the device restarts, and briefly waking the device and vibrating for them. It has no permission for the internet, the camera, your location, your contacts, your photos, media or your device's storage.

Camera, photos and files

You take a photo for a thought with your device's camera app; GedankenAnker receives only the finished picture. Photos and files you pick with Android's picker; the app gets only what you select. Files you share from other apps to GedankenAnker are copied into the app's storage. Photos can contain details such as the time and place they were taken; the app does not remove them, they stay in the photo on your device and travel with it when you share it. Only the reduced copy the app keeps ready for the device backup does not contain them (see below).

Reminders

Reminders and the morning or evening overview are scheduled and shown by the app on your device alone; there is no push service and no server. Without the notification permission the app works just the same, only without reminders.

Sharing, links and rating

When you share thoughts or attachments, Android opens its share sheet and you choose the receiving app; what you pass on there is processed by that app under its own privacy policy. Links in your thoughts open only when you tap them, in your browser or the app responsible for them; GedankenAnker loads no previews. The "Rate" card opens the app's page in the Play Store, to which Google's privacy policy applies.

Backup and transfer by Android

Android can include app data in the device backup of your Google account and restore it when the app is installed again, also on a new device. Whether this happens is up to you, in the Android settings under "Backup"; the app has no switch of its own for it. This backup is a service Google provides as part of your Google account under its own privacy policy; we are not involved in it and have no access to it. From Android 9 on it is end-to-end encrypted when your device has a screen lock, and it does not count towards your Google storage. You can delete it in the Android settings or in your Google account.

Into this cloud backup the app gives all your thoughts, lists, collections and settings, including completed thoughts and the trash. Android backs up at most 25 MB per app and nothing at all beyond that. To keep the backup below this, the app keeps copies of your attachments ready in a folder of its own, about 20 MB together with the thoughts, the newest attachments first: drawings as their strokes, from which the app draws the picture again when restoring; photos reduced in size (long side 1280 pixels) and without the time and place they were taken; other files (such as PDFs) unchanged if they are at most 1 MB in size. Larger files and older attachments that no longer fit are not in the backup; after a restore the app tells you once how many attachments were missing and removes them from the thoughts. Please keep photos and files you care about somewhere else as well.

When you set up a new device by transferring directly from device to device (Android 12 and later, by cable or Wi-Fi), everything comes along, including all attachments in their original size, photos with their details on the time and place they were taken. This transfer, too, is a feature of Android; the data only goes from one of your devices to the other.
