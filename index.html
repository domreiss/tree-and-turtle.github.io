Your thoughts stay with you

The app needs no user account, contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services, and has no servers of ours behind it. It has no permission to access the internet. We never receive what you write down in the app. Data leaves your device only when you yourself share it, or when Android includes it in your device backup (see "Android device backup").

Storage on your device

Your thoughts, lists, collections, dates, reminders and settings, as well as photos, files and drawings attached to thoughts, are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, which other apps cannot access unless you open or share something with them. This storage is strictly necessary for the app to work (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG); we ourselves do not process this data.

The data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings. An Android device backup is not affected; it is restored when you reinstall the app, until you delete it there.

Camera, photos and files

The camera app opens only when you choose to take a photo for a thought. Photos and files are picked through the Android system picker: the app receives only the items you select and has no permission to access your photos, media or storage. Files you share from other apps to GedankenAnker are copied into the app's storage. Photos can contain metadata such as the time and place they were taken; it stays in the photo and travels with it when shared or backed up.

Reminders

Notifications and reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. No push service and no server is involved.

Sharing and links

When you share thoughts or attachments, Android's share sheet opens and you choose the recipient app. From then on, that app's privacy policy applies. Links in your thoughts open in your browser or the app responsible for them; GedankenAnker itself does not load any previews from the internet. The "Rate" card opens the Play Store, where Google's privacy policy applies.

Android device backup

Depending on your device settings, Android can include the app's data in the device backup to your Google account and restore it when the app is installed again, also on a new device. This backup is run by Android and Google; we have no access to it. On Android 9 and later it is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock and does not count towards your Google storage. You control and delete it in the Android settings under "Backup" or in your Google account.

Purchase via Google Play

The app is bought through Google Play; it contains no in-app purchases. The seller and payment processor is Google (for buyers in the EU, EEA and the United Kingdom: Google Ireland Limited / Google Commerce Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland); Google is the controller for the purchase and your payment data, and Google's privacy policy applies (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive your payment details (payment method, billing address). For each purchase, Google provides us with the order number, date, product, amount, taxes, currency, device model and your country; for purchases from countries where we, not Google, act as the seller (outside the EU, EEA and UK), also city, region and postcode. To handle refunds or support requests, Google may additionally make your name and email address available to us. We use this information to handle refunds and requests (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), to evaluate our sales (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and to meet our tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). We keep the sales reports for eight years from the end of the respective calendar year (Section 147 of the German Fiscal Code, Section 14b of the German VAT Act); details of individual requests are deleted no later than twelve months after they are resolved. Beyond that we see only aggregated, non-personal statistics (such as installs per country), public reviews under the name you chose with Google and, if you allowed it in Android, anonymised crash reports.

Contact

If you contact us (e.g. by email), the information you provide is processed solely to handle your request (legal basis: legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; in the case of contractual inquiries, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and deleted no later than twelve months after your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). The authority competent for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de. Since the app does not send us your data, these rights mainly concern email communication with us and orders.

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to that processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You are not obliged to provide us with any data; without your email address we simply cannot answer a request. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

Changes to this privacy policy

We update this privacy policy whenever changes to the app require it, for instance when features that process data are added. The current version is always available at this address.

Links in Ihren Gedanken öffnen sich in Ihrem Browser oder der zuständigen App; GedankenAnker selbst lädt keine Vorschauen aus dem Internet. Die Karte "Bewerten" öffnet den Play Store; dort gilt die Datenschutzerklärung von Google.
