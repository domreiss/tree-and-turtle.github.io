The app does not collect any data

The game works completely offline and does not require an internet connection. The app does not use an internet permission of its own, does not contain advertising, does not use analytics or tracking services, and does not require any user account. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The only exception: making and restoring in-app purchases requires your device to be connected to the internet — this communication takes place exclusively between your device and Google Play, not with us (see section "In-app purchases via Google Play").

Local storage on your device

Your game progress (solved puzzles, settings, hint usage) is stored exclusively locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android system settings. We have no access to it.

In-app purchases via Google Play

The app offers optional one-time in-app purchases (unlocking of cases). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Payment data (e.g. payment method, billing address) is processed exclusively by Google; we never receive this data. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play as to whether a purchase has been made, in order to unlock the purchased content on your device (legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. b GDPR). For details, please refer to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

If you contact us (e.g. by email), the information you provide will be processed solely to handle your request (legal basis: legitimate interests, Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. f GDPR; in the case of contractual inquiries, Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. b GDPR) and deleted once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Rights of data subjects

Under the GDPR you have in particular the right to information (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). Since the app itself does not process any personal data, these rights are primarily relevant for email contact with us and for purchases processed by Google.

Changes to this privacy policy

We will adapt this privacy policy as soon as changes to the app make this necessary — for example, if data processing functions are added. The current version can always be found at this address.

Die App bietet optionale einmalige In-App-Käufe an (Freischaltung von Fällen). Die App erhält von Google Play lediglich eine Bestätigung, ob ein Kauf getätigt wurde, um die gekauften Inhalte auf Ihrem Gerät freizuschalten (Rechtsgrundlage: Vertragserfüllung, Art. 6 Abs. 1 S. 1 lit. b DSGVO).
